title: Offer a smaller first step
category: Conversion
alt: A hero headline with buttons underneath. Before, only a filled Book a demo button. After, an outlined Take the tour button sits beside it.
---

Many visitors (especially first-time visitors) might be interested in what you have to offer but aren't yet ready for a big commitment like booking a sales call. If that’s the only option on the page, they may simply leave the site and go to a competitor.

Offering a smaller first step alongside the main action, like a free guide, a product tour or a free trial can reduce risk and collect leads. People who are still deciding can take that step, and it keeps them in touch with you until they’re ready. Style it as a secondary button or a text link, so the main action still stands out for visitors who are ready now.
