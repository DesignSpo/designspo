---
title: Add reassurance to your CTA
category: Copy
alt: A call-to-action card reading Ready to build your website? Before, the button just says Sign up, leaving people to guess whether they’ll be charged. After, the button says Start your free trial and a small line under it reads No credit card required.
---

Visitors often hesitate the most right before they make a decision. This is especially true if the CTA is unclear. For example a “signup” button doesn’t necessarily tell them if they’ll be charged right away, if it’s a free trial they can cancel, or if they’ll start getting spammed with promotional emails.

Rewriting the CTA or adding reassurance near the CTA can reduce this hesitation. For example, rewriting the signup button to say “Start Your Free Trial” with a bit of text that says “no credit card required” is much more effective at getting signups.
