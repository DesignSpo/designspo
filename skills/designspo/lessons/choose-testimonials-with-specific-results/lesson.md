title: Choose testimonials with specific results
category: Conversion
alt: A testimonial card from Daniel Reyes, Head of Support at ACME, with an avatar beside his name. Before, the quote is vague praise that reads Great team, highly recommend. After, it names a result and reads Support tickets dropped 40% in two months, with 40% in blue.
---

A testimonial like “Great team, highly recommend” is pleasant to read, but it’s also not particularly memorable. It could describe almost any company, so it doesn’t attach a specific benefit to your product or service.

A testimonial that names a result tends to work much better. For example, “Support tickets dropped 40% in two months” tells readers exactly what results you helped someone get, and the number makes it easier to believe. That’s why it helps to ask customers for numbers or a before and after when you collect testimonials. Asking what changed after they started working with you often draws out the specific answer you’re looking for.
