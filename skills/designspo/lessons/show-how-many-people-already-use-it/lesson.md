Building trust close to CTAs is one the main ways to increase your conversion rates.

A simple bit of text like “used by 3,000 dentists” or “trusted by 50+ startups in SF” is a quick form of social proof that shows that people already trust you. It can fit right under a headline, a button, or a contact form and it’s close in proximity to the CTA so it won’t get ignored.

This kind of proof tends to work best when it’s specific to your audience. For example, “100 VC funds” means more to the right market than “10,000 investors”. It tells a specific kind of visitor that high signal people already trust you. It’s also important to update this number as it grows and to be as specific as possible. “126 businesses launched” is better than “50+ businesses launched” for example.
