A testimonial is only as believable as it is specific. A quote with no name, face, or title attached looks fake even if it's real.

Most strong testimonials are specific. They should include a quote about a specific outcome, the person’s name, their role and/or company, and a real photo. For example, “We cut onboarding from two weeks to three days” does more work than “Great product, highly recommend.” "From Ryan Johnson, CEO @ Acme" is much more believable than "From Ryan".
