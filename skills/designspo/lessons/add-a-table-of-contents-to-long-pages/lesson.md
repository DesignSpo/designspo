---
title: Add a table of contents to long pages
category: Navigation
alt: A long guide page on a website. Before, the article sits alone in the middle of the page, with no way to jump to a section. After, the article moves to the right and a table of contents on the left lists Goals, Sitemap, Content and Launch, with Sitemap highlighted.
---

Long pages, like guides, documentation and policies, are hard to navigate for most visitors. Readers usually only need to find one specific part, and Control F might not return what they’re looking for.

A table of contents at the top of the page, or one that stays in view at the side on wide screens, helps with this. List each main section as a link, so readers can jump straight to what they’re looking for.
