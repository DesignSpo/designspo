---
title: Balancing images and text
category: Hierarchy
alt: A card with an image and the headline Our story. Before, a large, dark image overpowers a small, pale headline. After, a smaller, lighter image sits beside a large, dark headline.
---

Images tend to get noticed first in a design. We call this “visual weight”. A large, high-contrast image has a lot of visual weight. But sometimes, images are only meant to support or add context to your copy. And next to a small, low-contrast headline, the image overpowers it and visitors skip the text entirely.

Before you place an image and a headline together, decide which one should lead. If the headline matters more, make the image smaller, lighter or lower in contrast, and give the headline more size and weight. If the image is the main focal point, like with product photography, let it lead and make the text shorter in length, smaller, or lower contrast.
