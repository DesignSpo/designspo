---
title: Place trust signals near the next step
category: Conversion
alt: A signup page for ACME with an email field and a Start free trial button, then a row of features and a footer. Before, the trust signals, We never share your email and a five-star 4.9 from 1,200 reviews rating, sit at the far end of the footer, past the rest of the page. After, the same two signals sit right under the button.
---

People tend to hesitate most right before they commit, like when they’re about to submit a form or enter their card details. When those small doubts show up, such as whether their data is safe or whether the product really works, it’s best to use some trust signals.

A security badge, a review rating, or a short guarantee placed beside the form or checkout button can answer those doubts. In contrast, the same signals tucked away in the footer usually do much less, since visitors aren’t looking at the footer while making a decision.
