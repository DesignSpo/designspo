---
title: Add a skip to content link for articles
category: Navigation
alt: A small website with a header and main content. Before, keyboard focus lands on the logo and has to pass every header link. After, a focused Skip to content link appears above the header.
---

People using a keyboard to move through a website press tab to navigate. For blogs, recipes, or dense writing they’ll sometimes have to press tab a dozen times before they can actually read your article. A simple “skip to content” button early on the page fixes this problem. If you think it messes with the flow of the design, you can even hide it until someone presses tab.
