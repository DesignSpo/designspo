---
title: Animate elements using short distances
category: Interaction
alt: A notification card with a dashed outline marking where its entrance starts. Before, the outline is 96px below the card. After, it is just 16px below, and the card fades in as it moves.
---

When an element slides in from far away, like a card flying up from the bottom of the screen, people have to watch it travel before they can use it. Likewise when a hover animation brings the element too far up, people have to track with their eyes. Long movements take more time, and they can pull attention away from whatever the person was doing.

Keep most animations short, somewhere between 4 to 24px. For example, a card might move up 4px while on hover. An image might slide in 24px from the right with a fade on entrance. The short shift still shows where the element came from or where it went, without messing up the focus of the visitor.
