title: Align elements to edges
category: Layout

When visitors scan your website, their eyes naturally follow an invisible line as they scroll. This line, we call an edge, should define where your headings, paragraphs, images, and other elements are aligned.

When different sections or elements are aligned to different edges, the eye has to work harder and your website looks messier. To fix this, pick two or three edges and align everything on the website to those. For example, in a two-column layout, the headings, paragraphs, and button are aligned to the left edge while the image is aligned to the right edge. Be careful though because padding, margins, or things like center-aligned text can throw this off if you've added it accidentally.
