---
title: Use urgency only when it’s real
category: Conversion
alt: An offer card for a six-week Landing Page Bootcamp at $249 with a Save my spot button. With a fake timer, the deadline reads Offer ends {current time + 10 hours}, a made-up deadline that moves with every visit. With a real deadline, the same spot reads Early pricing ends Oct 31.
---

When done well, urgency can increase conversions by pushing people to act now instead of later. For example, a countdown timer or a label that says “only 2 left” gives people a reason to buy. But the urgency has to be real for it to be effective.

But it can backfire (and is immoral!) if that urgency is fake. A deadline that resets every time the page reloads or a fake number of slots left means visitors who notice stop believing your other claims too. It’s better to use urgency only for real limits, like an event date, a price increase or a group program that’s actually filling up. For example, “Early pricing ends Oct 31” is a specific and true claim, and it gives people a real reason to act without tricking them.
